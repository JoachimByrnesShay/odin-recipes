Chicken Marsala

Description

Chicken breasts in sauce of Marsala wine, cream, and mushrooms

Ingredients

  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 4 skinless boneless chicken breasts, pounded to 1/2 inch thick
  • 1 cup sliced fresh mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onion
  • 1/3 cup dry Marsala wine
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1/8 cup milk
  • freshly chopped parsley, for garnish
  • lemon wedges for serving

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in large skillet, medium heat. Add chicken, and cook 10 to 15 minutes until golden brown on both sides and no longer pink in the center
  2. Add mushrooms and green onions to skillet, saute about 5 minutes until lightly browned. Add Marsala wine. Bring to a boil.
  3. Stir in cream and milk cooking over low heat until just heated through. Do not boil. Season to taste with salt and pepper and add garnishes.
