4 Ingredient Ramen Mac and Cheese

Description

Ramen noodles in cheddar sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1- 3oz package instant ramen noodles, seasoning packet set aside
  • 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1 green onion, sliced

Steps

  1. Bring water to boil in large saucepan. Set seasoning packet aside; add noodles to water and boil for 3 minutes. Strain noodles in colander and run under cold water. Set Aside.
  2. Add milk, cheese, and seasoning packet to saucepan. Cook over med-high heat while stirring often, until cheese is melted and sauce has thickened, about 3 minutes.
  3. Turn heat off. Add noodles back to saucepan and stir to combine
  4. Serve and top with sliced green onion
